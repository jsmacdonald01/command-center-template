# School calendar — fills the Coursework box

**What it gives you:** the Coursework box on the Home page lists your
graded work — assignments, quizzes, discussions, exams — with the day each
is due, soonest first. Tick one off when it's done. The day something
*opens* is never shown as the day it's due.

**What it shares:** nothing. The app reads your school's calendar link
directly. The link is stored encrypted on your computer and never shown
again.

**Cost:** free.

---

## Find your school's calendar link

Most school websites have a private "calendar feed" (also called iCal or
subscribe) link:

- **Brightspace / D2L:** open **Calendar** → **Subscribe** (or the gear
  icon) → copy the link.
- **Canvas:** open **Calendar** → **Calendar Feed** (bottom right) → copy.
- **Blackboard:** **Calendar** → the gear or **Share Calendar** → copy the
  iCal link.
- **Moodle:** **Calendar** → **Export calendar** → choose events and a time
  span → **Get calendar URL** → copy.

Treat the link like a password: anyone with it can see your schedule.

## Add it

1. In the app: **Settings** (on the Log page) → **School calendar**.
2. Paste the link → **Save**. It says how many things are coming up.
3. Click back into the app, and the Coursework box fills in.

No school? Leave it blank — the box just says nothing is connected.
