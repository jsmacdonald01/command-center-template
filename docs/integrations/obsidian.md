# Obsidian — your notes in the app

**What it gives you:** a notebook on the Workshop page that shows your
notes right in the app, with links between notes working. The agent and
Claude can read your notes too, to answer questions. Editing happens in
Obsidian — one click away with **Edit in Obsidian**.

**What it shares:** nothing. Notes stay on your own computer.

**Cost:** free. No account needed.

---

## What happens on its own

The first time Command Center starts, it makes a notebook for you in
`Documents\Command Center Notes` with a few starter notes, and shows it on
the Workshop page. You don't have to do anything for that.

## Install Obsidian (to edit notes)

1. Get Obsidian from **obsidian.md** (or let Claude Code install it for
   you — just say yes when it asks).
2. Open Obsidian once, then close it.
3. Close and reopen Command Center. It adds your notebook to Obsidian's
   list by itself, so next time Obsidian opens, your notebook is there.

That's it. In the app, click any note, then **Edit in Obsidian** to change it.

## Already use Obsidian?

In Command Center: **Settings** (on the Log page) → **Notebook** →
**Change folder**, and pick your existing notebook folder.

## The handoff notes

The notebook has a `Handoffs` folder. Each time you work on your Command
Center with Claude Code, Claude reads `Next Session.md` first, and at the
end you can say **"write a handoff"** — Claude updates it with what changed
and what's next. Every session picks up where the last one stopped.
